# Increment Specification

## Behaviour

- Define the observable behaviour this increment introduces or changes.

## Contracts

- Define API, CLI, file, event, schema, or integration contracts.

## Inputs / Outputs

- Inputs:
  - Describe accepted inputs and their source.
- Outputs:
  - Describe produced outputs and their destination.

## Invariants

- List conditions that must always hold true.

## Edge Cases

- List boundary conditions and unusual but expected states.

## Failure Semantics

- Define how failures are detected, surfaced, retried, or rolled back.
